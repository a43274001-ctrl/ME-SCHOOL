About Us

Faith Academy Ikorodu is committed to high quality education, spiritual growth and creativity development among students.

Our mission

Excellence, spirituality, creativity

Rooted in the Living Faith Church community in Ikorodu, we combine rigorous academics with strong Christian character formation, giving every student a firm foundation for life beyond the classroom.

To raise future leaders through excellence in education and Godly values, one student at a time.
